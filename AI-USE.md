# How AI was used in this repository

**What Claude (Anthropic's AI assistant) did**, working from my files and under my direction:
- Went through my folders (about 580 files) and the official RobotEvents record, and flagged gaps, duplicates and inconsistencies for me to resolve.
- Translated and summarised the team's Chinese documents (the engineering notebook, the season improvement record, the build guides) into English.
- Drafted the structure and the text of the pages from those documents, the code and my answers to its questions. I checked and corrected them.
- Read the code across its dated versions to show how it grew and changed.
- Processed files without changing their content: downscaled the photos inside the PDFs to keep each file under 10 MB, merged the 2023–24 build-guide chapters into one PDF per robot version, extracted renders and photos from the PDFs, and cropped a screenshot to remove a file path.

**What is not AI-generated:**
- The robots, the CAD, the code and the engineering decisions. They belong to the team, our mentor and me, as broken down in [ROLES.md](ROLES.md).
- The engineering notebook and design documents, written by our team and mentor in Chinese. They are published as originals.
- The events and results. Each one comes from our documents, the code, the official competition record, or my own account.
- The reflections on the [leadership page](leadership.md). They are my own words, written as answers to interview questions and edited only lightly.
