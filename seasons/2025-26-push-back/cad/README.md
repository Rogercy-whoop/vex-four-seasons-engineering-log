## Custom parts (in this repository)

These are the parts we drew ourselves, as opposed to VEX library parts. They open in SolidWorks or the free eDrawings Viewer.

## Assemblies

The full assemblies, including the Gen 3 robot (`V2-RG`) and the descore arm, are not committed. They are large (up to 28 MB), and they only open together with VEX's part library, which VEX distributes itself ([VEX CAD resources](https://v5rc-kb.recf.org/hc/en-us/articles/9636477857687-CAD-for-VEX-Robotics-Competition-Teams)).
